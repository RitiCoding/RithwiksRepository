story:
As lead mechanical architect for FRC 2583, I was responsible for the full robot layout and electromechanical integration.

The major subsystems included drivetrain geometry, multi-stage elevators, differential and linkage manipulators, compliant intakes, and high-torque endgame mechanisms made to take repeated shock loads.

I owned the CAD in Onshape: bearing fits, chain and belt center-to-center spacing, gearbox packaging, tube and plate structures, and tolerance stack-ups. Structured prototyping drove the decisions, with intake compression mapping, elevator deflection testing, and impact trials.

Fabrication ran across CNC routing, milling and turning, sheet metal, and additive, with serviceability designed in so repairs fit inside a five-minute pit window across 10+ events.
