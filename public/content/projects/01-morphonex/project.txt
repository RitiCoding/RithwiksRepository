story:
MORPHONEX is a complete quadruped that I modeled in Onshape and SolidWorks, from the frame and articulated legs down to the drivetrain and the mechanisms that let the body reconfigure itself.

A LiPo battery feeds a 24V rail that drives high-torque gimbal motors over a CAN loop to a Teensy 4.1. A Raspberry Pi 5 sits on a 5V rail for compute.

I derived the inverse kinematics it needs to walk and balance on its own, then tuned each motor's PID controller for positioning accuracy, response time, stability, and disturbance correction.

The reconfigurable body lets it adjust its stance for different terrain, and the forearm manipulators let it work with objects.
