story:
This was a research project into what a robot actuator can do when you design the whole stack yourself.

Each one covers the geartrain architecture, differential and linkage mechanisms, bearing selection, thermal management, and a housing built for tight packaging.

Motors, encoders, drivers, and microcontroller sensing are integrated into rigid, EMI-controlled assemblies.

I validated them with torque characterization, backdrive resistance measurement, and stress and fatigue testing, then kept refining under real robot load profiles.
