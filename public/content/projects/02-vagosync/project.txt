story:
VagoSync is a transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation (tVNS) wearable. It's a closed-loop neuromodulation prototype that combines sensing, embedded control, power regulation, isolation, and biphasic pulse generation.

I designed a custom electrical architecture that produces very precise biphasic pulses at precise intervals, and captured it in two hierarchical KiCad schematics with root and subsystem sheets.

The two custom two-layer boards were routed by hand, checked with DRC, ERC, and SPICE simulations, and hand-soldered, ICs included.

A SAMD21 reads biopotential, temperature, and GSR sensors at the same time. The firmware estimates changes in autonomic state and adjusts the stimulation output in real time.
