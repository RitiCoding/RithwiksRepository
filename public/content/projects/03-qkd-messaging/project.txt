story:
The question behind this project: how well does quantum key distribution work inside a real secure messaging system?

I built a quantum field simulation in Python and translated a theoretical quantum cryptography algorithm into working code, including key generation, transmission, error detection, and eavesdropping detection.

To make the physics fast enough to be useful, I vectorized the simulation with NumPy and cached intermediate states. PyCryptodome handles the encrypted communication on top of the simulated key exchange.

It took 2nd place in Systems Software at both the Greater Austin and Texas Science and Engineering Fairs.
